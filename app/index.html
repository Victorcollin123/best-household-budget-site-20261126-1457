Goals — five things the money is for

A budget without goals is just a list of limits. Each goal has a target, a monthly amount from the budget, and a date it will be done. The emergency fund comes first — everything else waits behind it.

How goals are ordered

1. Emergency fund — three months of essential spending, because one broken fridge or one hospital visit must never become a loan. 2. Rent deposit — saved monthly so January never hurts. 3. School fees — term fees saved before the term starts, never borrowed. 4. Inverter — the one goal that pays for itself, by ending the generator bill. 5. Holiday — last, because it is a want, and wants wait.

Method — where every figure comes from

The household

The Okafors are a worked example, not a real family: two adults, two children (9 and 6), a two-bedroom flat in Surulere, Lagos. Combined take-home pay ₦850,000 a month — salaries ₦700,000 and weekend catering income ₦150,000. Their budget, transactions, goals, debts and subscriptions are one consistent set of books: the Tracker rows add up to the Overview totals, the grocery basket multiplies out to the food budget, and the subscription audit adds up to the subscriptions line.

Prices & assumptions

  • Market prices are Lagos prices in November 2026: petrol ₦1,050 a litre, rice about ₦1,750 a kilo, a 40 GB data bundle ₦12,500.
  • The 50/30/20 rule is the target; the Okafors land at 68/7/25 because rent (₦170,000) and school fees (₦85,000) are fixed. The Planner page shows the gap honestly instead of pretending the target was met.
  • The grocery basket is one week for four people at ₦29,375; a month is 4.33 weeks, giving ₦127,194 — the ₦127,500 food budget.
  • The generator burns about 1 litre an hour; the inverter set is ₦850,000 with a five-year life and ₦2,000 a month upkeep.
  • Debt interest is charged monthly on the remaining balance, then minimums and the extra payment are applied — the payoff calculator simulates month by month, it does not use a formula.
  • Cash flow for December is projected; January to November is the year's record, November to the 26th.

What this site does not do

It gives no investment advice and no loan offers. It stores nothing on a server — added transactions live in your own browser. Figures are a worked example for a Lagos household in November 2026; your rent, your prices and your pay will differ, and the calculators are there so you can put your own numbers in.

About this site

The Household Ledger is an original site built for the make.fun bounty "Make the best household budget site". It runs offline from its own files — no network request, no build step, no tracker. Charts are hand-drawn on canvas; there are no libraries.
